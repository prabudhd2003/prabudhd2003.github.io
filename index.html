About

// the short version

I'm a master's student in Computer Science at USC Viterbi, on the Artificial Intelligence track. My work sits at the seam between multimodal machine learning and decision science: I like problems where the signal is spread across audio, video, and text, and where getting the model right actually changes what somebody does on Monday morning.

Most recently I built a multimodal transformer from scratch that predicts the exact moment a two-person conversation tips from calm into conflict — aligning asynchronous tone, facial expression, and language to hit 86.7% accuracy, about 7.2 points ahead of a Gemini API baseline. Before that I spent summers at Novartis and ZS / Pfizer doing the less glamorous, more consequential version of the same thing: gradient-boosted targeting models, RAG agents with a deterministic calculation layer, and resource allocation across 16 portfolios.

I've published twice with Springer (time-series forecasting; transformer-based summarization & sentiment), TA'd USC's graduate AI course, and I still write competitive-programming C++ for fun. Currently looking for Summer 2027 internships and new-grad research/ML roles — and seriously considering a PhD in multimodal learning.
